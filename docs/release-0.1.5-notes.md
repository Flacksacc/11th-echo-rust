Echo 0.1.5 adds local CPU post-processing after dictation stops. It includes configurable written-form rules for numbers, currency, dates, times, measurements, identifiers, email addresses and web addresses, plus optional offline punctuation restoration and sentence capitalization. Spoken email addresses now support multiple words before and after "at", including compound domain endings. The live transcript remains the original speech output. The punctuation model is downloaded only after confirmation and its installation progress is shown.
