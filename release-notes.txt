Echo 0.1.6 improves final transcript formatting.
- Replaced the previous punctuation-only model with a much smaller local CPU model that jointly restores sentence punctuation and word capitalization after you stop dictating.
- The new model is English-only, runs fully offline, and downloads only after confirmation. Existing protected names, addresses, and numbers remain protected.
- The live transcript remains raw while you dictate; formatting is applied once to the complete transcript after Stop.
